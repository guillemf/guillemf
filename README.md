# Guillem Fernandez

### Engineering Director · People Engineer · Author of *The Engineering Protocol Stack*

I build engineering organizations that ship — and people who want to stay.

25+ years across startups, scale-ups, and global enterprises, most recently
as **Head of Product Development at Advance 52 (G+D)**, following **Head of
Engineering at Bumble** and **Head of Transformation & Operations at Allianz
Technology**. I treat the human side of engineering with the same rigor as
the technical side, because in most organizations they turn out to be the
same problem seen from two different angles.

## What I do

Three lenses on the same problem:

- **Leadership — Team & org design.** Building high-performance teams from
  scratch, or untangling fragmented setups into a single, high-trust
  delivery engine, across time zones and cultures.
- **Architecture — Legacy to modern.** Leading transformations from
  monolith to microservices, defining multi-year roadmaps that balance
  delivery speed with long-term technical health.
- **Craft — Quality & practice.** Embedding CI/CD, TDD, and quality-first
  practices so reliability and speed stop being a trade-off — and start
  compounding.

## The book

**[The Engineering Protocol Stack](https://leanpub.com/engineeringprotocolstack)**
— a field guide to the human protocols behind every engineering
organization: how trust, communication, and process layer on top of each
other the same way CPU, LAN, and WAN layer on top of each other in a network
stack. Written for engineering leaders who want their teams to actually
ship.

Free essays, videos, and a self-assessment tool built on the same framework:
**[engineeringprotocolstack.com](https://www.engineeringprotocolstack.com)**

## What I'm running right now

[`engineeringprotocolstack.com`](https://github.com/guillemf/engineeringprotocolstack)
is the bilingual Jekyll site above, backed by a small stack of Rust
microservices — layered architecture, TDD, GitHub Actions CI/CD, deployed to
Amazon EC2 — that power the self-assessment tool and the consultation
booking flow:

- `eps-router_svc` — API gateway routing requests to the backend services *(private)*
- `eps-questions_svc` — self-assessment quiz engine *(private)*
- `eps-calendar_svc` — availability and booking-request service *(private)*
- `eps-calendar_admin` — Ratatui terminal admin tool for the calendar service *(private)*

It's the same engineering discipline the book argues for, applied to my own
project — happy to walk through the architecture on a call.
